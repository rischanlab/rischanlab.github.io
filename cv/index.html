Professional Summary

Dr. Rischan Mafrur is a lecturer at Western Sydney University Indonesia (WSUI), Surabaya, where he teaches Data Science and Big Data Applications. He earned his PhD in Data Science from The University of Queensland, Australia. He is a data professional with over 10 years of experience, specialising in Python, SQL, and data warehousing. His research and applied work sit at the intersection of financial data engineering, AI systems, and digital asset analytics, supported by active collaborations across Australia and Southeast Asia.
